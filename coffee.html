Coffee is a tool.

People will use it as fuel, a way to socialize, a way to make profit.
For me, the ritual of making coffee symbolizes a way to keep learning.
It is a place for me to fail alone.
Each cup will teach you a different lesson every time.
Instant results that are verifiable and shareable.
Sharing failures with others is a difficult thing.
Its easier when you can brew them a better cup right after ;)

Here you will find some latte art, cool beans, pourover stuff, and other coffee stories.
